What Pip is doing

    How it works

    Pip starts on its charging dock and knows nothing about the apartment. Each lidar sweep marks the cells a beam passes through as free and the cell it hits as a wall. That builds the map on the right.

    Exploring: Pip drives to frontiers, where known floor meets the unknown, until none are left. Paths come from A* on the map with walls inflated by Pip's radius, followed with pure pursuit and re-planned every second, so dropped boxes get routed around.

    Docking: below 25% battery Pip drives to a point in front of the dock, finds the dock's flat face in the lidar scan with a RANSAC line fit, lines up on its centre line, and creeps onto the contacts. If it misses, it backs off and tries again.

    This page runs the same algorithms as the ROS 2 packages in the repo, ported to JavaScript. To stay light it uses Pip's true position. The ROS version uses slam_toolbox and Nav2 in Gazebo.
