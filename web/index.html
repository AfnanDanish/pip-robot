Pip Source

A small robot that maps your home and puts itself to bed

This is Pip, running live. It starts on its charger knowing nothing, explores until the whole apartment is on its map, wanders, and drives home when the battery gets low. Drop boxes in its way or send it somewhere. The numbered signs are me explaining why things are the way they are.

Pip's day so far

    Under the hood

    Every lidar sweep marks the cells a beam passes through as floor and the cell it hits as wall. That's the whole map. Pip heads for the edges of what it knows, plans with A* around walls padded by its own radius, and re-plans every second, which is why a box you drop just gets driven around.

    Docking doesn't use the planner at all past a point. It looks for the dock's flat face in the scan, lines up on its centre line, and creeps the last bit blind. Signs 4 and 5 have the long version.

    The real Pip runs the same ideas on ROS 2 (Nav2, slam_toolbox, Gazebo for now). The code, the robot model and the build notes are on GitHub.
